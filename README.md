# Hi, I'm Abiodun Emmanuel Oyediran 👋

### GIS & Remote Sensing Specialist | Forest Biometrics | Geospatial Developer

I am a forestry and geospatial professional with a **BSc in Forestry** and an **MSc in Forest Biometrics and Information Systems** from the University of Ibadan.

My work sits at the intersection of **forestry, Earth observation, GIS, remote sensing, and computational modelling**, with a focus on using spatial and field data to understand and monitor forest ecosystems.

I am currently expanding my skills from **GIS analysis into geospatial development**, building reproducible workflows, spatial applications, and data-driven tools for environmental and forestry applications.

## 📌 Featured Projects

### 🌲 Forest Diameter Distribution Modelling

Statistical modelling of tree diameter distributions using forest inventory data.

**Focus:** Forest biometrics • Probability distributions • Model fitting • Cross-validation • R

---

### 🛰️ Forest Remote Sensing & Biomass Assessment

Analysis of satellite-derived spectral and vegetation indices with field inventory data for forest biomass and carbon assessment.

**Focus:** Landsat • Remote sensing • GIS • Biomass modelling • Regression • R

---

### 🗺️ Land Use/Land Cover Change Detection

Geospatial analysis of historical satellite imagery to investigate changes in forest cover and landscape composition.

**Focus:** Google Earth Engine • Landsat • LULC classification • Change detection

---

### 🌳 Forest Inventory & Biodiversity Analysis

Reproducible R workflows for analysing forest inventory datasets, including species diversity, structural characteristics and diameter distributions.

**Focus:** R • Forest inventory • Biodiversity • Statistical analysis

---

### 💻 Geospatial Applications

Building web-based and computational tools that combine spatial data, remote sensing and environmental datasets.

**Focus:** Python • JavaScript • GIS APIs • Spatial databases • Web GIS
