I am a **GIS and Remote Sensing Specialist** with a background in **Forestry and Forest Biometrics**, interested in using geospatial technologies, Earth observation data, and computational modelling to understand and monitor forest ecosystems.

My work combines **field inventory, satellite remote sensing, UAV data, GIS, statistical modelling, and programming** for forest monitoring, biomass and carbon assessment, land-use/land-cover change detection, and sustainable forest management.

I am currently expanding my work from **GIS analysis into geospatial development**, building reproducible workflows, spatial applications, and data-driven tools for environmental and forestry applications.

## 📂 Featured Projects

### 🌲 Forest Diameter Distribution Modelling

Statistical modelling of tree diameter distributions using forest inventory data, probability distributions, model evaluation, and cross-validation.

**Tools:** R • Forest Biometrics • Statistical Modelling

---

### 🛰️ Forest Remote Sensing & Biomass Assessment

Analysis of satellite-derived spectral and vegetation indices with field inventory data for forest biomass and carbon assessment.

**Tools:** Landsat • Google Earth Engine • GIS • R • Remote Sensing

---

### 🗺️ Land Use/Land Cover Change Detection

Analysis of multi-temporal satellite imagery to investigate changes in forest cover and landscape dynamics.

**Tools:** Google Earth Engine • Landsat • GIS • Remote Sensing

---

### 🌳 Forest Inventory & Biodiversity Analysis

Reproducible workflows for analysing forest inventory datasets, including species diversity, forest structure, diameter distributions, and tree growth characteristics.

**Tools:** R • Forest Inventory • Biodiversity Analysis

---

### 💻 Geospatial Applications

Development of web-based and computational tools that combine spatial data, remote sensing, environmental datasets, and interactive mapping.

**Tools:** Python • JavaScript • Web GIS • Spatial Databases
